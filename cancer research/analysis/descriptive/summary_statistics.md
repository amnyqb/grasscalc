**Effect-metric families: 12 incommensurable families across 13 populations.**
ERR per dose (Gy), ERR per dose (Sv), dose-response RR/HR, mortality ratio,
incidence-rate ratio, relative risk (×2), crude-rate ratio, ASIR change, modeled
lifetime risk, policy-presumptive list, community-survey ratio, "no incidence yet".
→ **Nearly one metric family per population — this is the quantitative reason the
evidence cannot be pooled** and is synthesized structurally (SWiM) instead.

## One-paragraph read-out
The evidence base comprises **63 references** (53 cited; only 2 with verified
identifiers) supporting **13 comparator populations** across **12 exposure
categories**, reported in **12 incommensurable metric families** — so the base is
structurally heterogeneous *by design*, which is precisely why it is synthesized
descriptively rather than pooled. **High-grade (A) evidence concentrates in
radiation and solvent-contamination cohorts**; combustion/dioxin sit at B;
disaster/conflict cases at F; petrochemical and policy as contextual (E/D). Only
**one** cohort carries individual smoking control. The **Saudi Eastern Province**
enters as a single **ecological** hypothesis whose crude 5.69× lung contrast
cannot be reproduced by realistic regional smoking differences — defensible at the
**Compatible** level and no further.
